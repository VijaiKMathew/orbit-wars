Core Engineering

Key Algorithmic Breakthroughs

How analytical geometry and vectorized simulation overcome real-time execution constraints in complex continuous multi-agent environments.

📐 Analytical Orbital Root Solver

Replaced 320ms discrete multi-step physics simulations with exact (1)$ trigonometric root-finding. Given target planetary angular velocity $\omega_t$ and orbital radius $, the arrival equation $\| ec{r}_t(t) - ec{x}_0\| = v_{ ext{ship}} \cdot t$ is solved in under \,\mu ext{s}$.

⚡ 16-Step Vectorized Lookahead

Evaluates candidate fleet dispatches across an explicit 16-turn forward timeline. Models garrison production replenishment, incoming third-party hostile fleets, and opportunistic 4-player snipe opportunities.

🛡️ Transit Return Defense

Prevents catastrophic back-door captures by tracking friendly and hostile fleets in flight. Reserves local garrison troops when a planetary garrison is threatened within $\Delta t \le 8$ turns.

⏱️ Dynamic 60s Overage Time Banking

Rather than allocating a uniform 100ms per turn, the engine runs at 8-12ms during trivial consolidation turns and banks time to spend up to 450ms during critical multi-front fleet collisions.

Engineering Rigor

Failure Autopsies: 5 Critical Anti-Patterns

Documented failure modes, empirical regressions, and structural post-mortems across 1,445+ tournament iterations.

Anti-Pattern 1: Greedy Nearest-Neighbor

Symptom: Dispatching fleets to the nearest Euclidean planet without accounting for angular movement.

Autopsy: Because planets rotate at distinct angular velocities, a target planet often swings away, resulting in a 40-turn wasted transit while home bases get captured.

Anti-Pattern 2: Discrete Step Integration

Symptom: Stepping Euler physics step-by-step (=0.1$) to find intercept times.

Autopsy: Consumed 320ms per turn (64% of total time budget), leading to platform timeouts and random dispatches under turn latency pressure.

Anti-Pattern 3: Total Garrison Depletion

Symptom: Sending 100% of planet ships to capture a high-value outer planet.

Autopsy: Left home planet at 0 ships; third-party opponents immediately sniped the base with a single ship, seizing 50 turns of accumulated production.

Anti-Pattern 4: Ignoring Transit Timers

Symptom: Evaluating planet ownership solely on present state, ignoring in-flight fleets.

Autopsy: Dispatched fleets to conquer planets already targeted by overwhelming friendly reinforcements, causing redundant resource waste.
