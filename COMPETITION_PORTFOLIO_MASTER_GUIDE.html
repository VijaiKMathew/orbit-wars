Core Architecture

Key Algorithmic Breakthroughs & Decision Pipeline

Comprehensive pipeline combining analytical root-finding, candidate pruning, 16-step forward state rollout, and transit return defense.

📐 Analytical Orbital Root Solver

Eliminated 320ms discrete integration loops (=0.1$) by deriving the exact closed-form collision equation. Given target planet angular velocity $\omega_t$ and orbital radius $, the ship intercept equation $\| ec{r}_t(t) - ec{x}_0\| = v_{ ext{ship}} \cdot t$ is solved via root-finding in under \,\mu ext{s}$.

⚡ 16-Step Vectorized Forward Rollout

Evaluates candidate fleet dispatches across an explicit 16-turn forward timeline. Models garrison production replenishment (0$ to 0$ ships/turn), third-party arrival conflicts, and multi-agent resource frontiers.

🛡️ Transit Return Defense

Prevents catastrophic back-door snipes by tracking friendly and hostile fleets in flight. Automatically enforces home-base defense garrison buffers whenever hostile fleets arrive within $\Delta t \le 8$ turns.

⏱️ Dynamic 60s Overage Time Banking

Instead of allocating a rigid 100ms per turn, the agent completes low-variance consolidation turns in 8-12ms, accumulating overage budget to allocate up to 450ms during decisive multi-front combat encounters.

Engineering Rigor

Failure Autopsies: 5 Critical Anti-Patterns

Documented failure modes, root causes, and structural fixes ablated across 1,445+ tournament experiments.

⚠️ Anti-Pattern 1: Greedy Euclidean Nearest-Neighbor
Symptom: Dispatching fleets to the closest Euclidean planet on the 2D grid.
Autopsy: Because planets rotate along concentric Keplerian orbits, target planets swing away during transit, stranding ships for 40+ wasted turns while home planets get captured.
Fix: Implemented (1)$ analytical collision equation solving for time-dependent intercept coordinates.
⚠️ Anti-Pattern 2: Discrete Step Integration (Euler Trap)
Symptom: Stepping forward at =0.1$ turns to detect orbital fleet arrivals.
Autopsy: Consumed 320ms per turn (64% of total time budget), inducing platform timeouts and random fallbacks.
Fix: Derived analytical trigonometric root solver cutting calculation time to <12μs.
⚠️ Anti-Pattern 3: Total Garrison Depletion
Symptom: Committing 100% of planet ships to capture high-value outer planets.
Autopsy: Left base garrisons at 0 ships; third-party opponents immediately sniped the base with a single ship, seizing 50 turns of accumulated production.
Fix: Enforced dynamic garrison buffers based on local transit threat proximity.
⚠️ Anti-Pattern 4: Blind Transit Timers
Symptom: Evaluating planet ownership solely on present state, ignoring in-flight fleets.
Autopsy: Dispatched fleets to conquer planets already targeted by overwhelming friendly reinforcements, causing redundant resource waste.
Fix: Tracked full transit vectors to model arrival timelines and defense buffers.
